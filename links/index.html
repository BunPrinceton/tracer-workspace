Curated Links

A collection of example 3D Neuroglancer scenes shared by the team — things worth a look across our datasets (ZFish, Pinky 100, Minnie, and Fly). Grouped by dataset, each entry opens a self-contained view or a saved scene you can explore.

Compiled from Ryan's shared sheet. Many of these are older links that may need restoration before they load — if one is dead, use the Restore step below.

Link won't load? Older saved scenes (appspot / json_url / nglstate links) can go stale. Use the Restore button on an entry — it copies the link and opens the FlyWire Link Restorer, where you paste it in to revive the scene.
