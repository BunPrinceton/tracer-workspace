Tool Evolution Timeline

The arc of how our annotation tooling has evolved across three eras of connectomics work.

Era 1: Citizen Science
Eyewire Era
Browser-based gamified neuron reconstruction for public participation. Pioneered crowdsourced connectomics.
Eyewire Browser-based
Era 2: Professional Desktop
VAST + Omni Era
Professional desktop tools for annotation (VAST) and 3D proofreading (Omni). Required Ubuntu workstations with local data access.
VAST Omni Ubuntu/Linux
Era 3: Web-Based Collaboration (Current)
WebKnossos + Neuroglancer Era
Web-based tools with collaborative features, AI assistance, and cloud integration. CAVE provides versioned annotation backend.
WebKnossos Neuroglancer CAVE FlyWire
