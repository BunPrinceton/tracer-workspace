Cell Segmentation Visual Glossary (82 figures)

Visual reference for hand-painting cell segmentations: how to handle myelin (padding rules with axons, neighboring cells, and myelin tongues), fat globules, in-plane and parallel membranes, semantic labels for somas, glia, blood vessels, and mitochondria, plus common imaging defects like invaginations, cracks, folds, and missing sections. Released v1.0 (owner: Selden Koolman).

Extracted from the archived recreation: Cell Segmentation Visual Glossary · original is the lab's Google source.

Optic Lobe Cell Name Guide (58 figures)

Naming reference for FlyWire optic-lobe cell types — Distal Medulla (DM), Transmedullary (Tm) and Transmedullary Y (TmY) cells (including Tm5a/b/c and TmY16/18), Medulla Tangential (Mt), Translobula (Tl), Lobula Columnar (LC / Lcn / Lccn), Optic Lobe Tangential (OLT), and related visual projection neurons. Each cell type carries literature citations (Takemura et al, Shinomiya et al, Krzysztof Kruk diagrams).

Extracted from the archived recreation: Optic Lobe Cell Name Guide · original is the lab's Google source.

Fly Synapses (29 figures)

FlyWire synapse-recognition reference (re-sync 2026) by Claire McKellar, Kyle Willie, Ben Silverman, Jay Gager, and Edna Normand. Examples of vesicle clusters, typical and atypical T-bars, presynaptic dots and postsynaptic targets, and other synapse anatomy as seen in the auto-detected FlyWire synapse layer.
